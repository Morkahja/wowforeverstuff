# WoW Forever: Talent Design Atlas

**All nine original classes | 27 talent trees | 466 talents | October 2026 beta snapshot**

A collection of our class-by-class talent design reviews, converted into one GitHub-friendly Markdown document. The central question: **does a talent change how a character plays, or mostly change the numbers?**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This is a **subjective mechanical-interest review**, not a talent power ranking or live-server verification. The descriptions and classifications reproduce our October 2026 discussion and the accompanying PDF. Beta abilities and tooltips may change, and some effects still require in-game confirmation.

## Criteria

- **Interesting (mechanical):** Introduces a new ability, meaningful proc, interaction between abilities, tactical decision, or conditional effect.
- **Numerical (efficiency):** Mainly raises stats, damage, healing, range, duration, or lowers resource costs and cooldowns.

Both can be strong or useful. These categories describe the *kind* of progression they offer, not their effectiveness.

## Design principle

**Not necessarily more powerful talents. More talents that give players new things to do.** Interactions, decisions, combat reactions, and moments of discovery can make spending a talent point feel like learning something. Numerical improvements still have a place, but shouldn't be the only form of character development.
